# E4-12 — Listen and Sort

Create a standalone single-file English Grade 4 listening game.

Learning objective: identify familiar words and phrases from audio and classify them by topic.

Mission: listen, then sort the heard word into the correct topic zone.

Gameplay: topics include food, school, family, animals, hobbies, places and daily routines. Play a browser-generated or bundled pronunciation/audio cue and show 3–4 topic zones.

Control: POINT/DRAG. Optional MediaPipe fingertip tracking with confidence >= 0.65, smoothing and 300ms drop cooldown. Mouse/touch fallback.

Generate at least 60 word items and 20 short phrases. Randomize zone order and recycle missed items. Do not reveal the target word before the learner answers.

Feedback replays the audio, reveals the word, gives a picture/definition and uses it in a short sentence.

Provide replay speed, captions only after answer, camera-off mode, no upload. Output one HTML file, no TODOs.
