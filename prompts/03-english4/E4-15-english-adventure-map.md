# E4-15 — English Adventure Map

Create a standalone single-file Grade 4 English review game.

Learning objective: integrate vocabulary, listening, spelling, sentence building and basic grammar.

Mission: travel across five map islands by completing one English skill challenge at each island.

Gameplay: Vocabulary Village, Listening Lake, Spelling Forest, Sentence Bridge and Grammar Castle. Each island uses a distinct mechanic and ends with one review question.

Generate at least 75 tagged questions with adaptive recycling of mistakes. Keep vocabulary and grammar age-appropriate.

Optional camera controls may use fingertip pointing/swiping, with calibration, confidence >= 0.65, smoothing and cooldown. Mouse/touch/keyboard fallback is mandatory.

Every wrong answer produces a short explanation and reappears later.

Include progress map, stars based on mastery rather than speed alone, reduced motion and camera-off mode. No video upload. Output one complete HTML file, no TODOs.
