# E5-11 — Reading Detective

Create a standalone single-file English Grade 5 game.

Learning objective: read short passages and identify main idea, detail, sequence, meaning from context and simple inference.

Mission: investigate a mystery by collecting evidence from a short passage.

Gameplay: display 120–180 word age-appropriate passages. Ask one question at a time and let the learner highlight/select the evidence sentence before choosing an answer.

Use POINT selection; optional webcam fingertip tracking with confidence >= 0.65, smoothing and 300ms selection cooldown. Mouse/touch fallback.

Generate at least 30 passages across school, nature, science, hobbies, travel and everyday life. Randomize options. Include distractors based on a real sentence but wrong inference.

Feedback highlights evidence and explains why the correct choice fits.

Add read-aloud, adjustable font, camera-off mode and no upload. Output one complete HTML file, no TODOs.
