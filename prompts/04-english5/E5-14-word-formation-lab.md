# E5-14 — Word Formation Lab

Create a standalone single-file English Grade 5 game.

Learning objective: recognize and use word families and common prefixes/suffixes at an upper-primary level.

Mission: transform a base word into the form needed to complete a sentence.

Gameplay: present a base word and a sentence gap. The learner chooses or assembles the correct related word. Use child-friendly transformations such as happy → unhappy, help → helpful, quick → quickly only where pedagogically appropriate.

Generate at least 60 items with clear answer keys. Include traps from wrong suffix, wrong part of speech and spelling changes.

Primary control: POINT/DRAG with optional fingertip tracking, confidence >= 0.65, smoothing and 300ms cooldown. Mouse/touch fallback.

Feedback shows base word, transformed word, part of speech and sentence meaning.

Include accessibility, camera-off mode and no upload. Output one complete HTML file, no TODOs.
