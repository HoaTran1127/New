# T5-13 — Volume Vault

Create a standalone Grade 5 Math single-file HTML game.

Learning objective: calculate volume of rectangular/cuboid shapes and connect cubic units with dimensions.

Mission: unlock vaults by building a box with the requested dimensions and computing its volume.

Gameplay: show length, width and height. The player adjusts three dimension controls or selects the correct volume card. Include unit labels and visual unit cubes.

Optional webcam control: two-hand distance/gesture to increase or decrease one dimension. Use smoothing, confidence >= 0.65, 250ms control debounce and clear release state. Mouse/touch fallback.

Generate 40 problems with varied dimensions and units. Include traps: area instead of volume, forgetting one dimension, multiplying incorrectly and mismatched units.

Feedback visually fills the cuboid with unit cubes conceptually and shows V = l × w × h.

Use 10-round sessions, difficulty progression and review of missed concepts. Camera optional, no upload, large controls. Output one complete HTML file, no TODOs.
